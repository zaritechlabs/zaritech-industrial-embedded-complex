[HIGH-FREQUENCY TRANSIENT DECOUPLING ARCHITECTURE]

 1. HIGH-SPEED POWER INTEGRITY DECOUPLING LOOP (Gürültü Filtreleme):
    - External MCU VCC (+3.3V / +5V) ---> Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - Ceramic Filtering Capacitor Lead 1 ---> Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - Ceramic Filtering Capacitor Lead 2 ---> Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - External MCU Ground (GND)       ---> Breadboard Power Rail GND
    * Note: Seating the ceramic capacitor directly adjacent to the power entry 
            points minimizes high-frequency line impedance and dampens power ripples.

 2. BALANCED PASSIVE PULL-DOWN SWITCHING CIRCUIT:
    - Breadboard Power Rail VCC ---> Tact Button Pin 1A
    - Tact Button Pin 1B         ---> Targeting MCU Digital GPIO Pin (Input Mode)
    - 1k Ohm Precision Resistor  ---> Seated between Tact Button Pin 1B and GND Rail
    * Note: Establishes a rigid, hardware-debounced pull-down logic baseline.

 3. CURRENT-LIMITED DIAGNOSTIC SIGNAL PATH:
    - Targeting MCU Digital GPIO Pin (Output Mode) ---> 330R Resistor Lead A
    - 330R Resistor Lead B ---> Visual LED Anode (+)
    - Visual LED Cathode (-) ---> Breadboard Power Rail GND
