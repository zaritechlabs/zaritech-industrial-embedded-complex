[COMPUTATIONAL-FREE ASYNCHRONOUS FLUID ALARM CIRCUITRY]

 1. DC POWER REGULATION HARDWIRING:
    - Main DC Power Source Positive (+) ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC (+5V)
    - Main DC Power Source Negative (-) ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND (0V)

 2. SOLID-STATE TRANSDUCTION SWITCHING LOOP (Su Baskını Alarm Hattı):
    - Water Level Sensor VCC Pin   ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - Water Level Sensor GND Pin   ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - Water Level Sensor Signal    ---> 1k Ohm Base Resistor Lead A
    - 1k Ohm Base Resistor Lead B  ---> 2N2222 Transistör 1 Base (B) Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Collector---> Active Buzzer Positive (+) Terminal
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Emitter  ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - Active Buzzer Negative (-)   ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC [Low-Side Sinking]
    * Note: Fluid contact bridges nickel sensor traces, shifting the 2N2222 BJT 
            instantly into saturation mode to drive the active buzzer alarm.

 3. COMPLEMENTARY DUAL-LED STATE MATRIX:
    - Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC ---> Red Status LED Anode (+) via 330R Resistor
    - Red Status LED Cathode (-)     ---> 2N2222 Transistör 2 Collector Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 2 Base (B)   ---> Connected parallel to Transistör 1 Base
    - 2N2222 Transistör 2 Emitter    ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    * Note: Acts as a physical parallel diagnostic bus rail to synchronize 
            visual alerting states with the acoustic warning threshold.
