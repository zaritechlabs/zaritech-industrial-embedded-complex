[COMPUTATIONAL-FREE ASYNCHRONOUS THERMAL FAN INTERLOCK CIRCUITRY]

 1. DC POWER REGULATION HARDWIRING:
    - 18650 Battery Holder Positive (+) Wire ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - 18650 Battery Holder Negative (-) Wire ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail GND

 2. AUTOMATIC THERMAL OVERRIDE LOOP (Akıllı Termostat Hattı):
    - NTC Temperature Sensor VCC Pin ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - NTC Temperature Sensor GND Pin ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - NTC Temperature Sensor DO/AO   ---> Precision Base Resistor (1k) Lead A
    - Precision Base Resistor (1k) Lead B ---> 2N2222 Transistör 1 Base (B) Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Collector  ---> KY-019 Relay Module Control INPUT (IN) Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Emitter    ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail GND
    * Note: When temperature crosses the multi-turn trimpot threshold, the sensor logic 
            shifts, injecting current into the BJT base to engage the low-side relay coil.

 3. ELECTROMECHANICAL FLUID PROPULSION POWER PATH:
    - KY-019 Relay Module VCC Terminal ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - KY-019 Relay Module GND Terminal ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail VCC---> KY-019 Relay Common (COM) Screw Terminal
    - KY-019 Relay Normally Open (NO)  ---> 3V-6V High-RPM DC Motor Terminal A (+)
    - 3V-6V High-RPM DC Motor Terminal B (-) ---> 400-Pin Breadboard Power Rail GND
    * Note: Closes the high-current circuit to instantly drive the fluid propeller fan.
