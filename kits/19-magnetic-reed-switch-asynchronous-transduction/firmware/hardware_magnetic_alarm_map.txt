[COMPUTATIONAL-FREE ASYNCHRONOUS MAGNETIC ALARM CIRCUITRY]

 1. DC POWER DISTRIBUTION REGULATION:
    - Main DC Power Source Positive (+) ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC (+5V)
    - Main DC Power Source Negative (-) ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND (0V)

 2. SOLID-STATE TRANSDUCTION SWITCHING LOOP (Manyetik Kontak Alarm Hattı):
    - Reed Switch Module VCC Pin    ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC
    - Reed Switch Module GND Pin    ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - Reed Switch Module Signal Out ---> 1k Ohm Base Resistor Lead A
    - 1k Ohm Base Resistor Lead B   ---> 2N2222 Transistör 1 Base (B) Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Collector ---> Active Buzzer Positive (+) Terminal
    - 2N2222 Transistör 1 Emitter   ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    - Active Buzzer Negative (-)    ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC [Low-Side Sinking Mode]
    * Note: Magnetic proximity closes the hermetic contacts, injecting current directly 
            into the 2N2222 base rail to actuate the acoustic warning threshold.

 3. COMPLEMENTARY VISUAL DIAGNOSTIC LED MATRIX:
    - Mini Breadboard Power Rail VCC ---> Green Status LED Anode (+) via 330R Resistor
    - Green Status LED Cathode (-)   ---> 2N2222 Transistör 2 Collector Pin
    - 2N2222 Transistör 2 Base (B)   ---> Mapped parallel to Transistör 1 Base
    - 2N2222 Transistör 2 Emitter    ---> Mini Breadboard Power Rail GND
    * Note: Synchronizes the physical visual state indicators parallel to the buzzer output stream.
