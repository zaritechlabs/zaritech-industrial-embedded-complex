[ACTIVE SECOND-ORDER SALLEN-KEY LOW-PASS FILTER TOPOLOGY]

 1. DUAL-RAIL POWER INFRASTRUCTURE HARDWIRING:
    - External Split Power Supply (+) ---> TL074 Pin 4  (VCC+)
    - External Split Power Supply (-) ---> TL074 Pin 11 (VCC-)
    - External Split Power Supply GND ---> Mini Breadboard Unified Ground Rail (GND)

 2. ACTIVE SALLEN-KEY RC FILTER STAGE (Alçak Geçiren Filtre Ağı):
    - Raw Noisy Analog Signal Input  ---> Hassas Direnç R1 (10k) Lead A
    - Hassas Direnç R1 (10k) Lead B  ---> Hassas Direnç R2 (10k) Lead A
    - Hassas Direnç R2 (10k) Lead B  ---> TL074 Pin 3 (Non-Inverting Input 1IN+)
    - Junction (R1 Lead B / R2 Lead A)---> Precision Filtering Capacitor C1 (10nF) Lead A
    - Precision Filtering Capacitor C1 Lead B ---> TL074 Pin 1 (Output 1OUT) [Active Feedback]
    - TL074 Pin 3 (1IN+)            ---> Precision Filtering Capacitor C2 (10nF) Lead A
    - Precision Filtering Capacitor C2 Lead B ---> Mini Breadboard Unified Ground Rail (GND)

 3. UNITY-GAIN BUFFER INTERLOCK CONFIGURATION:
    - TL074 Pin 2 (Inverting Input 1IN-) ---> TL074 Pin 1 (Output 1OUT) [Direct Shunt]
    - Conditioned Clean Analog Signal Out Node ---> TL074 Pin 1 (Output 1OUT)
    * Note: Yields an isolated, low-impedance filtered output string ready for 12-bit ADC digitization.
